ST Hero Calculator — best gear and skills for Shop Titans

1. Unzip the folder.
2. Open en.html (English) or index.html (French) in your browser.
3. On first launch, click "Download the data". The page downloads the official
   "Shop Titans Data Spreadsheet" from Google Sheets (about 10 seconds).
4. The data stays in your browser.

Updates: the page checks the sheet version each time it opens, and once a day
if it stays open. When a new version exists, click "Update" (one click).
